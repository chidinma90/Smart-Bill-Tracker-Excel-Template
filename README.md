# Smart-Bill-Tracker-Excel-Template
The Smart Bill Tracker is a simple yet powerful Excel template designed to help individuals and businesses track recurring bills, avoid missed payments, and stay financially organized.
It automatically calculates due dates, days left, and bill status — all with clear visual indicators.

## Key Features

- Automated bill due date calculation
- Supports monthly, quarterly, bi-annual, and annual bills
- Auto-calculated days left to expiration
- Color-coded bill status:
  - Green – Active
  - Orange – Due Soon
  - Red – Expired
- Clean and easy-to-use layout
- Protected formulas to prevent errors
- Customizable bill frequency list


## Sheets Included

- Bill Tracker
- Drop-Down Lists
- Pivot Table and Bill Distribution Sheet


## User Guide

  ## Due Dates Column
  • Automatically updates based on Start Date and Frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, Bi-Annual).
 
  • Date format: YYYY/MM/DD (validated to prevent errors).

  ## Days Left Column
  • Automatically calculates days remaining before the bill’s due date.
  
  ## Status Column
  • Active: Green fill – bill still running.
  • Due Soon: Orange fill – bill due in 7 days.
  • Expired: Red fill – bill overdue.


## How it Works
- Enter the bill name, start date, frequency, and amount.
- The tracker automatically calculates the next due date.
- Days left and bill status update automatically.
- Visual alerts help you take action before bills expire.
